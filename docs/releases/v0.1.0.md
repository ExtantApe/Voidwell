First release. A OneBlock add-on for Paper 26.1 and later: walk in through the Waystation's OneBlock gate, ring your island's way bell three times to leave, and turn what the void takes into a meter that pays back powers, island visits and void-touched gear.

Needs Waystation 0.4.0 or later and OneBlock 1.6.4. Set OneBlock's `autojoin` and `rebirth_on_the_island` to false. See the README's Install section for the rest.

Tested with bots on throwaway Paper servers. It hasn't been seen in a real game client yet. The README lists what was and wasn't tested.
