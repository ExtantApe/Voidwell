The real fix for the respawn on the island after a bell trip, found with 0.1.1's trace on a copy of a real server.

## Fixed

- **A OneBlock death ends at the Waystation's OneBlock gate, even with Multiverse-Inventories restoring last locations.** With `apply-last-location-for-all-teleports` on, Multiverse-Inventories put a respawned player back on their last spot in the station, which after a bell trip was the step into the OneBlock gate, and the gate then took them to their island. Half a second after every OneBlock respawn, Voidwell now checks the player is within a block of the landing (0.1.1 only checked they weren't in OneBlock), and if not, puts them back there and says so in the console.
- Use it with **Waystation 0.7.0**, where a teleport that leaves a player at a gate can't let the gate take them on their first step.

## Upgrading

Replace the jar, and Waystation's with 0.7.0. No config or data changes; no server setting needs changing.

## Tested

Bot tests on a throwaway Paper 26.2 server with Waystation 0.7.0: the rehearsal's repro (bell trip, back, void death) respawns at the gate; when the console moves the player onto the island, or onto the step into the gate, right after the respawn, Voidwell puts them back at the landing and logs it. The first-visit, void-death and way-bell sections passed 21/21 (one earlier run had a health check off by 3 points, unrelated and not seen again). The whole suite wasn't rerun for this release.
