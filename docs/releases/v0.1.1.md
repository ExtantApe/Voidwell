A fix for one case where Voidwell's promise about deaths didn't hold.

## Fixed

- **A OneBlock death always ends at the Waystation's OneBlock gate.** On a rehearsal copy of a real server, a player who had rung their bell, gone back through the gate and then died in the void respawned on their island, while Voidwell told them "You're back at the Waystation". Voidwell sets the respawn at the gate, and in its own tests it lands there; something else on that server moved the player afterwards. Voidwell now checks half a second after every OneBlock respawn and, if the player has been moved back into OneBlock, puts them at the gate and writes a console warning saying where they'd been moved. Nothing was ever lost: the void meter charged correctly throughout.

## New

- **`-Dvoidwell.trace=true`**, for troubleshooting: logs every teleport and respawn with the code that caused it, so an admin can see which plugin moves players. Off unless set.
- The bot suite reports a failed set-up as a failure, instead of "0 passed, 0 failed".

## Upgrading

Replace the jar. No config or data changes.

## Tested

Bot tests on a throwaway Paper 26.2 server with Waystation 0.6.0, OneBlock 1.6.4, Multiverse and LuckPerms: the rehearsal's repro (bell trip, back, void death) respawns at the gate, and when the console moves the player back onto the island right after the respawn, Voidwell puts them at the gate and logs it. The whole suite passed 86/86 (82 before plus 4 new). Not run on 26.3 (bots can't stay connected there), and the plugin that moved the player on the real server isn't identified yet.
